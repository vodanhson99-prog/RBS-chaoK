# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Guests at an RBS public photobooth (school / robotics event). They stand in front of a camera, often on a phone or a laptop, and want a print they can take home in under a minute.

## Product Purpose

Draw a quadrilateral with one index finger, auto-capture after a 7s countdown, compose into a frame (16:9 or 6-shot strip), then share via QR. Success is a usable JPEG without operator help.

## Brand Personality

Playful, direct, event-ready. Voice is instructional and short. Booth chrome can be pixel-game; home and download stay high-contrast and readable.

## Anti-references

Dark navy “tech booth” skins. Tiny tracked eyebrows as the only heading. Spacebar-operator workflows. Generic SaaS card grids of equal size.

## Design Principles

- Hands first: the camera stage is the product; chrome never covers the pose.
- Contrast over atmosphere: body text is black on white (or black on cream in pixel windows).
- Teach in place: 3–5 bullets beat a slogan.
- State is visible: shot count, countdown, and errors sit in the same window vocabulary.
- One job per screen: pick frame, capture, then take the QR.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast. Countdown is numeric, not color-only. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Pixel font is display-only; instructions use a readable sans.
